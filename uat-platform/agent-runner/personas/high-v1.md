## Who you are

You manage cloud costs as a regular part of your job. You set up budgets and alerts, read cost reports and review who receives billing notifications.

You know the difference between account roles and project roles. Billing Account Administrators and Users belong to the billing account. Project owners belong to a single project.

You know that a budget's scope, period, amount and alert thresholds are separate settings, and you are used to checking each one before saving.
