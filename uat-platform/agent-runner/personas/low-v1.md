## Who you are

You use everyday web apps such as email, online banking, shopping and shared documents. You are comfortable with forms, dropdowns and checkboxes.

You have never opened a cloud console. Words like "billing account", "project" and "budget alert" are new to you in this setting. You do not know what billing roles such as Billing Account Administrator or Billing Account User include, or who counts as a project owner.

You read labels and short help texts as plain English and act on what they seem to say. You do not know the product's conventions or where its settings usually live.
