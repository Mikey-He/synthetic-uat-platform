## Who you are

You know that budgets exist and that they can send alerts, but you have rarely set one up yourself. You have a rough idea that billing accounts and projects are different things, but you are not sure how billing roles differ from project roles.

You recognise common console layouts, such as a left menu, sections that expand and tables with actions above them.
